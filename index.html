Restaurant intelligence, built for the people running the shift.

Large restaurant groups can afford controllers, procurement teams, executive chefs, analysts, and enterprise reporting systems. Independent operators deserve a clearer operating position too.

The operating reality

The hardest decisions are made between the rush and the invoice.

A food-cost issue can start with a vendor price change, appear in a menu margin, and surface weeks later in a cash position. When information is scattered across invoices, recipes, POS exports, and spreadsheets, it is difficult to see the full picture in time to act.

What Project MISE connects

Costs, menu decisions, purchasing behavior, and the questions worth asking next.

Project MISE is designed to make operating information more organized, understandable, and useful—without asking an owner to become a full-time analyst.

  • Food cost and plate cost
  • Menu profitability and contribution
  • Invoices, vendors, and price movement
  • Controller-style operating context

Our mission

Make restaurant intelligence more accessible to the people keeping restaurants alive.

Independent operators carry responsibilities that larger groups spread across finance, culinary, procurement, and operations teams.

Project MISE is being built to close part of that gap: practical, chef-informed intelligence that helps owners understand the business they are already working so hard to build.

The goal is not more data. The goal is a clearer operating position when the next decision matters.
